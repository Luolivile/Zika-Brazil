# Zika-Brazil
An exploratory analysis of Zika virus notifications in Brazil (2016–2025), examining national and regional trends in reported cases.

## Zika virus in Brazil (2016–2025)

### Data
Zika virus notification data were obtained from the Brazilian Ministry of Health
(SINAN), covering the period 2016–2025. Data were provided as annual CSV files and
combined for analysis.

### Methods
Exploratory analyses focused on annual notification counts aggregated at the national,
state, and macro-regional levels. Geographic analyses were based on the state of
notification. Agreement between state of notification and state of residence was high
(99.6%), indicating minimal geographic misclassification.

### Key findings
- Zika notifications peaked nationally in 2016, consistent with the initial epidemic.
- Following a sharp decline, low-level transmission persisted across all regions.
- The **North region exhibited a distinct post-epidemic pattern**, with higher and more
  variable notification counts compared with other regions.

### Notes
Counts for recent years may be affected by reporting delays and ongoing case closure.
